# Battle surface brief

- Scope: full-viewport playable battle on desktop and portrait mobile; visitor mode is Operate.
- Audience/job: a Collector must read both teams, choose an available Move or switch, then watch the complete uninterruptible action resolve.
- Primary task: advance the player Charge Strip, spend it on one of three predictable Moves, manage active Relics, and defeat the opposing Lineup.
- Required content: two independent Charge Strips, both Lineups and health states, active portraits, timer, class matchup, statuses, predicted Move values/costs, retry/reward states, blocking matchup and `3, 2, 1, FIGHT` beats, and fixed action-presentation locks. Difficulty, track and settings remain available through setup or pause rather than permanent live chrome.
- Constraints: semantic DOM controls; Phaser arena; reduced-motion fallback with identical simulation locks; no colour-only state; rectangle/square Kinetic Print language; full-stage portrait and landscape compositions.
- Chosen direction: a game-first full-screen arena using the owner's Teeny Titans 2 references for interaction hierarchy and LOFTWAH FIGHTER's rectangular Kinetic Print art for execution.
- Memorable moment: selecting a Move tears its printed label out of the lower tray, slams a full-width opponent-free cut-in across the arena, then returns to the drawer with the damage stamped onto the target.
- Pinned interaction/composition reference: the user-supplied reference-game battle screenshot, translated into loftwah/fighter's authored print style rather than copied literally.
- Composition inventory: arena fills the viewport; one compact opponent Health-and-Charge strip; edge-pinned Lineup portraits; large asymmetric fighter stills; three equally prominent rectangular Move controls attached to a long bottom Charge Strip; compact pause/timer controls; dedicated VS, countdown, action and result states.
- Raster inventory: arena, two canonical portraits and idle pairs, three Mara cut-ins, Mara reaction sheet, story scene, store art, tournament scene.
- Semantic/code inventory: navigation, labels, values, bars, icons, focus, statuses, buttons, type wheel, paper frame, motion, audio controls.
- Resolved in this pass: art-first Shared Fight Setup, portrait mobile composition, thin persistent HUD, dedicated VS and countdown beats, fixed presentation locks, generic cut-ins for every action, full-scene result treatment, and removal of persistent battle-log clutter.
- Unresolved: final title, final class names, final art approval, and whether future art production adds bespoke cut-ins for every opponent Move.
