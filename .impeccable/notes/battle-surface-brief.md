# Battle surface brief

- Scope: full-viewport playable battle on desktop and portrait mobile; visitor mode is Operate.
- Audience/job: a Collector must read both teams, choose an available Move or switch, then watch the complete uninterruptible action resolve.
- Primary task: advance the player Charge Strip, spend it on one of three predictable Moves, manage active Relics, and defeat the opposing Lineup.
- Required content: two independent Charge Strips, both Lineups and health states, active portraits, timer, class matchup, statuses, predicted Move values/costs, difficulty, track, settings, retry/reward states, blocking `3, 2, 1, FIGHT`, and fixed action-presentation locks.
- Constraints: semantic DOM controls; Phaser arena; reduced-motion fallback with identical simulation locks; no colour-only state; rectangle/square Kinetic Print language; full-stage portrait and landscape compositions.
- Chosen direction: an underground risograph fight bill organised as a pulled-open collector drawer.
- Memorable moment: selecting a Move tears its printed label out of the lower tray, slams a full-width opponent-free cut-in across the arena, then returns to the drawer with the damage stamped onto the target.
- Pinned interaction/composition reference: the user-supplied Teeny Titans 2 battle screenshot, translated into Riot Relics' authored print style rather than copied literally.
- Composition inventory: arena fills the viewport; top-corner health; edge-pinned Lineup portraits; large asymmetric fighter stills; three equally prominent circular Move seals attached to a long bottom Charge Strip; compact pause/timer/settings controls; full-field diagonal countdown and action cut-ins.
- Raster inventory: arena, two canonical portraits and idle pairs, three Mara cut-ins, Mara reaction sheet, story scene, store art, tournament scene.
- Semantic/code inventory: navigation, labels, values, bars, icons, focus, statuses, buttons, type wheel, paper frame, motion, audio controls.
- Resolved in this pass: portrait mobile composition, slow start-from-zero Charge pacing, blocking countdown, fixed presentation locks, generic cut-ins for every action, and removal of persistent battle-log clutter on compact screens.
- Unresolved: final title, final class names, final art approval, and whether future art production adds bespoke cut-ins for every opponent Move.
