# Battle surface brief

- Scope: desktop-first application shell and playable battle; visitor mode is Operate.
- Audience/job: a Collector must read both teams, choose an available Move or switch, and understand the result without pausing.
- Primary task: advance the player Charge Strip, spend it on one of three predictable Moves, manage active Relics, and defeat the opposing Lineup.
- Required content: two independent Charge Strips, both Lineups and health states, active portraits, timer, class matchup, statuses, predicted Move values/costs, difficulty, track, settings, retry/reward states.
- Constraints: semantic DOM controls; Phaser arena; reduced-motion fallback; no colour-only state; rectangle/square Kinetic Print language; mobile orientation remains undecided.
- Chosen direction: an underground risograph fight bill organised as a pulled-open collector drawer.
- Memorable moment: selecting a Move tears its printed label out of the lower tray, slams a full-width opponent-free cut-in across the arena, then returns to the drawer with the damage stamped onto the target.
- Approved north star: `.impeccable/mocks/battle-collector-drawer.png`.
- Composition inventory: wide central specimen arena; Lineup pull-tabs on opposing rails; health and Charge paired directly below active fighters; class matchup between them; three equally prominent Move labels in a strict bottom tray; compact story/music/settings rail above.
- Raster inventory: arena, two canonical portraits and idle pairs, three Mara cut-ins, Mara reaction sheet, story scene, store art, tournament scene.
- Semantic/code inventory: navigation, labels, values, bars, icons, focus, statuses, buttons, type wheel, paper frame, motion, audio controls.
- Unresolved: final title, final class names, portrait mobile composition, final art approval, exact long-term information density.
